About

Made on Cape Cod since 2005

Dan’s Clams makes stuffed quahogs — also called stuffies, or “cohogs” — along with lobster rolls and fried clam bellies.

Stuffed quahogs from Dan’s Clams have been sold at a market in Teaticket, in the Falmouth area of Cape Cod, and at other small local markets nearby.

This site is a new home for the business, so it’s easier to find the food and get in touch about orders. A few details (phone, email, hours, and exact shop names) are still being confirmed and are marked as placeholders below.
